The studio

A small studio that would rather ship than pitch.

Blue Swan Lake Studios is six people who have shipped fourteen apps together. We take on three or four products at a time, which is the only honest way to promise a build in your hand every Friday.

Calm on the surface

You get one point of contact, one weekly build, one changelog. No account-manager telephone game, no status decks about status.

Relentless underneath

Typed codebases, tests on the money paths, crash budgets, and a stabilisation window baked into every quote — not sold as an extra.

We say no

If your idea needs a bigger team, a different stack, or six more months than you have, we will tell you on the first call.

How we got here

2019

Two of us start taking on app builds nights and weekends. First Play Store release in November.

2021

Studio goes full time. Weekly-build rule introduced after one project drifts three months.

2023

Internal-tools practice added — the spreadsheets clients kept apologising for became half our work.

Today

Fourteen apps shipped, six people, three or four products in the water at a time. Two slots open for November.
